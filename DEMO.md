# Presentation and demo script

1. Business problem (45 s): an operations manager needs to distinguish a remote
   headline from an event that affects the company's location or dependencies.
2. Product (90 s): run the clearly labeled fictional demo, inspect evidence, choose
   Singapore, confirm the event, then switch the business to Rotterdam. Vary the
   radius and select a shipping dependency. Explain direct versus indirect exposure.
3. Model (90 s): show baseline and Transformer metrics side by side, training
   configuration, held-out splitting, parameter count, and checkpoint artifacts.
   Explain why synthetic smoke results do not establish quality.
4. Engineering (45 s): show requirement IDs, contracts, source-instruction isolation,
   failure handling, and tests. Demonstrate JSON evidence export.
5. Findings (60 s): present real-data results when available, limitations, errors,
   latency, and measured triage-time savings. Do not replace missing results with
   target metrics. Name the missing rubric section A.

For the final submission, add an independently annotated test set, model/data cards,
actual business validation, and a recording that shows successful live extraction.
